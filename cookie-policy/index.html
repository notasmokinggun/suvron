Cookie policy

Cookies: what this website sets, and what it doesn't

Last updated September 2026

This policy covers the SUVRON Money website at suvron.in, run by Shantilekh Global Technology Pvt. Ltd. It explains what the site stores in your browser, and what it doesn't.

The short version

This website does not set cookies. It has no analytics, advertising or social media trackers, and it does not use local storage or similar tools to follow you from page to page or from site to site. That is why you never see a cookie banner here.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website can save in your browser. Sites use them to remember a login or a language choice, to count visitors, and sometimes to follow people across other sites for advertising.

What this website does

The site is a set of static pages with no accounts and no login, so there are no session cookies. Every page loads only files that come from suvron.in itself: the pages, styles, a small script for menus and forms, fonts and images. The loan calculator runs inside your browser, and the numbers you enter never leave your device.

Like every website, the server that delivers these pages (GitHub Pages) receives standard technical details each time your browser asks for one, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested. Your browser has to send those for the page to load, and the host handles them under its own privacy practices. We don't add cookies to that exchange.

Fonts and other third parties

The fonts (Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, IBM Plex Mono and Noto Sans Devanagari) are hosted on suvron.in, so your browser doesn't contact Google or any other font service to display them. No third-party scripts, pixels or embedded widgets run on these pages.

Forms

The eligibility, Apply now and contact forms don't send anything to our servers. They open a draft email in your own email app with what you typed, and nothing reaches us until you press send there.

Links to other sites

Some pages link out to sites we don't run, such as the RBI's complaint portals and the Google Play Store. Those sites set their own cookies under their own policies, and those policies apply once you're on them.

The SUVRON Money app

This policy is about the website only. How the app collects and uses information is covered in our privacy policy.

Your choices

Because the site sets no cookies, there is nothing to switch off. If you'd like to check for yourself, your browser's developer tools list the cookies and storage a site uses (look for the Application or Storage tab). You can also block or delete cookies for any site in your browser settings, and this one will work the same either way.

If this changes

If we ever add analytics or another tool that uses cookies, we'll list it on this page first, with what it does and how long it lasts, and ask for your consent before it runs. The date at the top will change when we update this page.

Questions

Write to connect@suvron.in. If you have a complaint, the route to raise and escalate it is on our grievance redressal page.
