What we collect, and what we do with it

This is SUVRON Money’s privacy policy: what information we collect, why we need it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it.

Effective October 17, 2026

Version: PP/2.0/2026-27 Effective date: October 17, 2026 Replaces: version of January 2026 Policy owner: Shantilekh Global Technology Pvt. Ltd.

1. Who we are

SUVRON Money is a digital lending platform built and run by Shantilekh Global Technology Pvt. Ltd. (“SUVRON Money”, “we”, “us”). We operate the SUVRON Money app and the website suvron.in.

We don’t lend money ourselves. Loans are sanctioned and disbursed by our lending partner, Ready Leasings Private Limited, a non-banking financial company registered with the Reserve Bank of India (“our lending partner”). We collect your information through the app, check it, and pass to our lending partner what it needs to assess, approve and service your loan. Our lending partner makes the final lending decision.

This policy applies to everyone who uses the app or the website. It works alongside our Terms of Use, your loan agreement, and the Key Fact Statement you receive before accepting a loan. It is written to meet the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules, and the Reserve Bank of India’s directions on digital lending.

2. Your consent comes first

We collect personal data only for the purposes described here, and only after asking for your explicit consent in the app. Each permission on your phone is requested separately, with a short explanation of why we need it, before you grant it.

You can withdraw your consent at any time (see section 9). Withdrawing it doesn’t undo anything done lawfully before you withdrew, and some data must still be kept where the law requires it.

3. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, mobile number, email address, date of birth, gender, current address and PIN code.
  • Identity and KYC: your PAN, Aadhaar details (collected and verified in line with UIDAI’s rules), a live selfie or photograph, and proof of address where needed.
  • Work and income: whether you’re a student, salaried or self-employed, your employer or institution, and your monthly income.
  • Bank details: the bank account and IFSC you want the loan paid into and repaid from.
  • Documents you upload: such as bank statements or salary slips, if we ask for them to assess your application.
  • Support conversations: messages, emails and call recordings when you contact our support team, kept so we can resolve your issue and train our team.

Information from your phone, with your permission

  • SMS messages: the sender, content and time received of SMS messages on your device. We use them to understand your income, spending and existing repayments, which is a central part of how we assess your application. SMS access is required to apply for a loan. If you don’t grant it, we can’t assess or offer you a loan through the app.
  • Installed apps: a list of apps installed on your device, which we mainly use to identify other lending and financial apps. This helps us understand your existing borrowing and spot fraud.
  • Location: your device location, collected once, at sign-up, to confirm your address, check that we serve your area, and help prevent fraud. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Camera: used once, during KYC, to take the selfie and photographs needed to verify your identity.
  • Device information: your phone’s model, operating system and version, and app version, used to secure your account and stop unauthorised devices acting in your name.

The app does not access your contacts, call logs, or the photos, videos and files stored on your phone.

Information from using SUVRON Money

  • Loan and payment records: your applications, offers, loans, repayments, due dates and payment methods.
  • App usage: when you open the app, which screens you use, crashes and errors, so we can keep the app working and improve it.

Information from others

  • Credit information companies (credit bureaus), for your credit report and score, with your consent.
  • Identity and KYC sources, such as the Central KYC Records Registry and authorised government verification services, to confirm the details you give us. By applying, you consent to us and our lending partner fetching your KYC records from the Central KYC Records Registry using the details you provide.
  • Banks and verification providers, to confirm that the bank account you give us is yours and active.

4. Why we use it

  • To assess your application: verifying your identity, checking your eligibility, and working out the amount, tenure and price of any loan you’re offered. Our assessment uses automated models alongside our lending partner’s credit policy.
  • To run your loan: paying out the money, sending reminders, collecting repayments, and keeping your loan records.
  • To keep you and us safe: detecting and preventing fraud, identity theft and misuse of the app.
  • To support you: answering your questions and resolving complaints.
  • To improve the service: fixing problems and making the app easier to use, using aggregated or de-identified data wherever possible.
  • To meet the law: complying with RBI directions, anti-money-laundering rules, tax law, and lawful requests from regulators, courts and law enforcement. Transactions that appear suspicious are reported to the appropriate authority as the Prevention of Money-laundering Act, 2002 requires.

We don’t use your information for anything outside these purposes without asking you first.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only where it’s needed to provide the service, and only under written agreements that require the recipient to protect it and use it solely for that purpose:

  • Our lending partner, Ready Leasings Private Limited, which assesses, approves, funds and services your loan.
  • Credit information companies, to check your credit history and, as the law requires, to report how your loan is repaid.
  • KYC, verification and e-signing providers, to verify your identity and bank account and to let you sign your loan documents digitally.
  • Banks and payment providers, to send your loan to your account and collect repayments.
  • Technology providers, such as cloud hosting, data analysis and messaging services, who process data on our instructions.
  • Regulators and authorities, including the RBI, courts and law enforcement, where the law requires it.

If a loan is overdue and a recovery agent is involved, we share only what they need to contact you about that loan, and they must follow the RBI’s fair practices code.

6. Where your data is stored and how we protect it

Your personal data is stored on servers located in India.

We protect it with encryption in transit and at rest, strict access controls so only staff who need data can see it, activity logging, and regular reviews of our security practices. Everyone at SUVRON Money with access to personal data is bound by confidentiality obligations, and the people and companies we share data with are bound by contract to equivalent safeguards. We remain responsible for how they handle your data on our behalf. We do not store your debit or credit card numbers or CVV.

If a personal data breach happens, we will inform the Data Protection Board of India and the people affected, as the law requires, and tell you what we’re doing about it.

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires:

  • Identity, KYC and loan records are kept for at least five years after your loan relationship ends, as anti-money-laundering rules require.
  • App usage and device data is kept for a shorter period, unless we need it longer to investigate a security issue or fraud.
  • Other data is deleted or de-identified once it’s no longer needed, for example if you apply but never take a loan.

8. Automated decisions

Our models score loan applications automatically using the information described in section 3. If you think a decision about you is wrong, you can ask for it to be reviewed by a person by writing to us (see section 12).

9. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • Get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and who we’ve shared it with.
  • Correct or complete data that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date.
  • Ask us to erase your data, unless we have to keep it by law or to service a loan you still have.
  • Withdraw your consent to any use of your data. If you withdraw consent that a loan application depends on, such as SMS access, we won’t be able to process new applications, but we can still service an existing loan.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if you’re not satisfied with our answer, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, write to support@suvron.in from your registered email address. We may need to verify your identity first. We aim to respond within 7 days, and always within the time the law allows.

10. Messages from us

We’ll contact you by SMS, email, WhatsApp, app notifications or phone about your application and loan, including approvals, due dates and payment reminders. These service messages are part of running your loan.

You can turn off app notifications at any time in your phone’s settings, though you may then miss reminders about your loan. We’ll send promotional messages only with your consent, and you can opt out at any time from the app or by writing to us. We follow TRAI’s rules on commercial communication, including your Do Not Disturb preferences.

11. Children

SUVRON Money is only for people aged 18 or over. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we find that we have, we delete it.

12. Contact us

For questions about this policy or to use your rights, write to support@suvron.in.

If you’re not satisfied with our response, contact our Grievance Redressal Officer, Ms. Ekta G, at gro@suvron.in or 9971373543. Our full escalation process, including how to reach the RBI, is in our grievance redressal policy.

13. Cookies

The suvron.in website doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies. Our cookie policy has the details.

14. Changes to this policy

We’ll update this policy when our practices or the law change. When we make a significant change, we’ll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect, and where the change needs your consent, we’ll ask for it again. The version number and effective date at the top always show the current version.
