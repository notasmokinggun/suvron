# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students, first jobbers and freelancers or gig workers in India, aged 18 to 35, who need a small amount of money (Rs 2,000 to Rs 50,000) before their salary, stipend or next gig payment lands. Most arrive on a phone. Their job on the website: understand what SUVRON Money is, trust it, and install the Android app.

## Product Purpose

SUVRON Money is the technology platform (app, KYC, underwriting models, disbursal, support) behind short-term personal loans that RBI-registered NBFC partners sanction, disburse and collect. The website exists to turn a visitor into an Android app install. Success is installs from people who can afford the loan.

## Positioning

Small, short, upfront: Rs 2,000 to Rs 50,000 for 30 to 180 days, no collateral and no credit history needed, a decision within 24 hours, and every cost shown in a Key Fact Statement before the borrower accepts.

## Operating Context

- Apply only through the Android app (Google Play, com.shantilekh.suvron). No iOS app, no web application.
- In-app flow: mobile sign-up, PAN, Aadhaar OTP and a selfie (under three minutes), affordability check, offer with Key Fact Statement, money to the bank account within 24 hours, digital repayment.
- Lending partner named on the site: Ready Leasings Private Limited (RBI-registered NBFC).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Interest 10% to 36% a year; APR including fees 10% to 120%. Exact cost only in the Key Fact Statement.
- SUVRON Money does not lend money itself; that disclaimer must stay visible.
- Static HTML/CSS/JS site on GitHub Pages (suvron.in), no build step. Subpages: about, contact, calculator, blog, responsible-lending, grievance-redressal, privacy-policy, terms, cookie-policy.
- The homepage loan calculator is optional and may be cut (a /calculator/ page exists).

## Brand Commitments

- The logo mark is fixed: a blue S interlocked with an orange rupee sign (`SUVRON LOGO WITH NO BG.png`, `logo-128.webp`, icons). Name written "SUVRON Money".
- The current dark green and yellow site palette is not a commitment and may change.
- Voice: plain, direct, no hype ("Borrow only what you can repay").

## Evidence on Hand

- Google Play rating 4.3 from 249 reviews, with real dated reviews already on the homepage (keep).
- Referral: up to Rs 100 per friend who takes a first loan, no cap (keep).
- Ask an AI buttons (ChatGPT, Claude, Google AI Mode) and six FAQs (keep).
- Legal and NBFC disclosures, rate ranges, grievance officer gro@suvron.in / 9971373543 (keep).
- No customer counts, disbursal volumes, press or partner logos exist: never invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Cost before commitment: every number a borrower would ask about is on the page, nothing hidden.
2. Built for the phone first: the visitor is on a budget Android phone on mobile data.
3. Calm about money: reassure without hype, urgency tricks or gamification.
4. Compliance is part of the product, not small print to hide.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Budget Android phones on slow connections; keep pages light and readable. The grievance policy is published in five languages; Indian-script fonts (Devanagari, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada) are already loaded on the site.
