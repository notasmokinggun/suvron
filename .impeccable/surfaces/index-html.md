# Homepage (index.html)

Scope: the suvron.in homepage, full redesign (replacement visual world). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience and job: 18-35 students, first jobbers and freelancers in India, mostly on budget Android phones; understand the loan, trust it, install the Android app. Main action: Get the app (Google Play).
Must keep: reviews (4.3 from 249) and referral, Ask an AI + FAQ, legal and NBFC disclosures. Calculator may go.
Build path: code-led (no image generation available). Roll ran degraded (no catalog, no challengers). User delegated the direction choice ("whatever appeals to the audience"); the assigned direction was built.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is an Indian hand-painted shop sign: the loan's terms painted plainly on a rate board, the way a trusted local shop paints its prices. It refuses the category default of a dark gradient fintech hero with floating phone mockups and feature-card grids.

OWN-WORLD: Enamel blue sign boards (the logo's blue) with marigold-orange painted numerals carrying a darker painted shadow line, white enamel panels with navy lettering, a single pinstripe border on boards. Display face Yatra One (Mumbai sign-painting lettering); text Manrope. Cool white page ground, navy ink, orange the one action colour. Square sign-board corners.

STORY: Visitor learns what it is (small short loans for students and freelancers), sees every term and the cost on the board, sees the four app steps, learns who actually lends (the NBFC), reads real reviews, gets answers, installs.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left five columns: h1 in the painted face, a plain lead with the amount and 24-hour fact, Get the app plus See the rates, a rating line. Right six columns: an enamel blue board with a pinstripe, the app's offer screen (Key Fact Statement) cut by the board's bottom edge, one small card over its lower-left that loops Verified, Approved, Money sent.

FORM: Painted rate board, candidate 7 of 7 on my list (hand-painted signage), assigned by the roll. seed:04acaaf3abb2a7d9. Signature interaction: the rate board, whose painted numbers are the page's facts; the hero card loop.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
