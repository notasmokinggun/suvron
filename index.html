Questions people ask us

Can I apply without a credit history?
Yes. You need to be 18 to 35 with a valid PAN, Aadhaar and an active bank account. No existing credit history is required.
How much can I borrow, and for how long?
₹2,000 to ₹50,000, repaid over 60 to 180 days. Your exact amount and tenure come with your offer, before you accept.
Will I see the full cost before I accept?
Yes. Interest runs 10% to 36% per annum and the APR (interest plus all fees) 10% to 120%, depending on tenure, amount and credit assessment. Your exact APR is in your Key Fact Statement and loan agreement before you accept.
How fast is disbursal once I'm approved?
Most approved loans are disbursed within 24 hours, straight to the bank account you verify during sign-up.
Is my data private?
Yes. It's encrypted and used only to process your application. It's shared with our NBFC lending partners where needed for underwriting and compliance, never sold to advertisers or unrelated third parties. See our privacy policy.
What happens if I repay late?
Late fees apply and are listed in your loan agreement before you accept. Missed payments get automated reminders first, then a support agent if needed.

SUVRON Money is a digital lending platform built by Shantilekh Global Technology Pvt. Ltd. Loans are sanctioned, disbursed and collected by our RBI-registered NBFC partners, who retain final control over credit approval and terms. SUVRON Money does not itself lend money. Interest rates for loans facilitated on this platform range from 10% to 36% per annum, and the APR, inclusive of interest and all fees, ranges from 10% to 120%, depending on tenure, amount and your credit assessment; the exact APR for your offer is disclosed in your Key Fact Statement and loan agreement before you accept.
