Questions people ask us

Who can apply for a SUVRON Money loan?
Anyone between 18 and 35 with a valid PAN, Aadhaar and an active bank account can apply. You don't need an existing credit history to be considered.
How much can I borrow, and for how long?
Loans currently range from ₹2,000 to ₹50,000 with a tenure of 60 to 180 days. Your exact amount, tenure and total cost depend on your eligibility check, and you'll see all of it before accepting an offer.
What does a SUVRON Money loan cost?
Interest rates range from 10% to 36% per annum and the APR, inclusive of interest and all fees, ranges from 10% to 120%, depending on tenure, amount and your credit assessment. The exact APR for your offer is disclosed in your Key Fact Statement and loan agreement before you accept, never buried afterward.
How fast is disbursal once I'm approved?
Most approved loans are disbursed within 24 hours, straight to the bank account you verify during sign-up.
Is my personal data safe with SUVRON Money?
Your data is encrypted and used only to process your application. It's shared with our NBFC lending partners where needed for underwriting and compliance, never sold to advertisers or unrelated third parties. See our privacy policy for the full picture.
What happens if I repay late?
Late fees apply and are spelled out in your loan agreement before you accept. From the day a payment is missed, you'll hear from us through automated reminders and, if needed, a support agent, never a surprise call out of nowhere.
