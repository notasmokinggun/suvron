3. What SUVRON Money is, and isn’t

SUVRON Money is a digital lending platform. We act as a lending service provider for our Lending Partner: we let you apply through the app, verify your details, assess your application using our technology, and help service your Loan.

We don’t lend money ourselves. Every Loan is sanctioned, disbursed and owned by the Lending Partner, which makes the final decision on whether to lend, how much, and on what terms. Your Loan is a contract between you and the Lending Partner, governed by your Loan Agreement and KFS.

If anything in your Loan Agreement or KFS conflicts with these Terms, the Loan Agreement and KFS apply to that Loan.

We don’t give financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Information on the Platform, including our calculators and blog, is general information to help you decide for yourself.

4. Who can use SUVRON Money

To use the Platform you must:

  • be at least 18 years old, and within the age range our Lending Partner accepts (currently 18 to 35);
  • be a resident of India with a valid PAN, Aadhaar, and an active bank account in your own name;
  • be legally able to enter into a binding contract; and
  • complete KYC verification as required by the RBI.

Meeting these requirements doesn’t guarantee a Loan. Our Lending Partner can decline any application, including from someone who has borrowed through us before.

6. What we offer

Loan size: ₹2,000 to ₹50,000.

Tenure: 30 to 180 days.

Interest & charges: interest from 10% to 36% a year; the APR, including interest and all fees, from 10% to 120%, depending on amount, tenure and your credit assessment. Your exact figures are in your KFS and Loan Agreement before you accept.

Loans are unsecured, fully digital, and repaid within a fixed tenure. They’re meant for short-term needs. Borrow only what you can repay.

7. How a loan works

  1. Apply: sign in with an Aadhaar-linked mobile OTP, verify your PAN and complete KYC.
  2. Assessment: we and our Lending Partner assess your application, including a credit bureau check and the information described in our privacy policy.
  3. Offer: if approved, you see your offer with its KFS. Nothing is final until you accept it and sign the Loan Agreement electronically.
  4. Disbursement: the Lending Partner pays the Loan directly into your verified bank account.
  5. Repayment: you repay the Lending Partner on the dates in your KFS, through the methods shown in the app.

8. Changing your mind: the cooling-off period

After your Loan is disbursed, you have a cooling-off period to exit it. During this period you can cancel the Loan by repaying the principal and the proportionate APR for the days you held it, with no penalty. The length of the period is set by our Lending Partner, is at least 3 days, and is stated in your KFS. To use it, contact us at support@suvron.in or through the app.

After the cooling-off period ends, the Loan runs to its tenure under your Loan Agreement, including any terms it sets for repaying early.

9. Repayments and late payments

  • Repay in full and on time. We’ll send reminders before each due date, but missing a reminder doesn’t change what’s due.
  • If you pay late, the Lending Partner may apply penal charges. These are disclosed in your KFS and Loan Agreement. They’re a fixed charge, not added to your interest rate, and not compounded.
  • Your repayment history is reported to credit information companies, as the law requires. Paying late can lower your credit score and make future borrowing harder.
  • If you’re struggling to repay, tell us early. We’ll explain your options.

10. How we collect overdue payments

Collections follow the RBI’s Fair Practices Code and its rules on recovery. That means:

  • reminders by SMS, email, WhatsApp, app notification and phone, from the first missed payment;
  • if a recovery agent is involved, you’ll be told in advance who they are, and they must identify themselves and carry authorisation;
  • calls only at reasonable hours, and no threats, abuse, intimidation, or contact with your friends, family or employer about your debt; and
  • you can complain about any collection contact through our grievance redressal process.
