About SUVRON Money

We handle the plumbing, so a loan actually feels simple.

SUVRON Money is the technology behind the app: identity checks, credit models, disbursement, and a support team that answers. The loans themselves are funded by our RBI-registered NBFC partners.

Four things we’re built to get right

None of this is visible from the app screen, which is the point. It should just work.

Digital KYC

Aadhaar-linked mobile OTP sign-in and PAN verification, done in one sitting on your phone.

ML-based underwriting

Our models weigh a range of data points instead of leaning on a single credit score.

Digital disbursement

Funds move straight to your bank account. Repayment and collections stay digital too.

Compliance first

RBI Fair Practices Code, the DPDP Act, and RBI’s CEPC framework govern every step.

Compliance runs through every stage, not just the fine print.

Fair treatment under the RBI’s Fair Practices Code, data handled under the DPDP Act, and grievances resolved on a fixed timeline under the RBI’s CEPC framework. If something goes wrong, there’s a defined process, not a black hole.

SUVRON Money is a digital lending platform built by Shantilekh Global Technology Pvt. Ltd. Loans are sanctioned, disbursed and collected by our RBI-registered NBFC partners, who retain final control over credit approval and terms. SUVRON Money does not itself lend money. Interest rates for loans facilitated on this platform range from 10% to 36% per annum, and the APR, inclusive of interest and all fees, ranges from 10% to 120%, depending on tenure, amount and your credit assessment; the exact APR for your offer is disclosed in your Key Fact Statement and loan agreement before you accept. Borrow only what you can repay.
