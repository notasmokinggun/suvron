Why we exist

Built for the gap between paydays.

Students, first jobbers and freelancers often need a few thousand rupees before a stipend, salary or gig payment lands. Banks want a credit history they don’t have yet, and many loan apps show the real cost only after you’ve signed. We built SUVRON Money for that gap.

Every cost before you commit

Interest, every fee and the APR are in your Key Fact Statement before you accept anything.

Credit checks that look past one score

Our models weigh a range of information, so you don’t need an existing credit history to apply.

Small and short on purpose

₹2,000 to ₹50,000 for 30 to 180 days, with no collateral. Borrow only what you can repay.

Compliance runs through every stage, not just the fine print.

Fair treatment under the RBI’s Fair Practices Code, data handled under the DPDP Act, and complaints handled on fixed deadlines under the RBI’s CEPC framework. If something goes wrong, there’s a defined process, not a black hole.

How complaints are handled
  • RBI Fair Practices Code
  • DPDP Act data protection
  • RBI CEPC grievance redressal
  • Support replies in 7 days, our grievance officer resolves in 14 days

SUVRON Money is a digital lending platform built by Shantilekh Global Technology Pvt. Ltd. Loans are sanctioned, disbursed and collected by our RBI-registered NBFC partners, who retain final control over credit approval and terms. SUVRON Money does not itself lend money. Interest rates for loans facilitated on this platform range from 10% to 36% per annum, and the APR, inclusive of interest and all fees, ranges from 10% to 120%, depending on tenure, amount and your credit assessment; the exact APR for your offer is disclosed in your Key Fact Statement and loan agreement before you accept.
