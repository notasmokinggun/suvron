Interest rates for loans facilitated on this platform range from 10% to 36% per annum, and the APR, inclusive of interest and all fees, ranges from 10% to 120%, depending on tenure, amount and your credit assessment; the exact APR for your offer is disclosed in your Key Fact Statement and loan agreement before you accept.
